# v7-dev — rail, dock, removable media, textured materials

v7 replaces the v6 top bar with the left rail and top dock, adds removable media and
clipboard history, turns the reading modes into presets over options, and replaces every
flat stand-in on the cards with the foil, patina and dither materials from the swatch pages.
It follows DESIGN.md §6 and §8, DESIGN-SYSTEM.md §3.5a–§3.13 and SURFACES.md §2a.

**Nothing in v7 has run under Quickshell yet.** This build environment has no Qt runtime,
qmllint or Quickshell. What was checked, and how, is listed under *Verification*. Treat it as
a development build: run it side by side (`qs -p`) and keep Waybar as the fallback.

## What changed

**Materials (all cards).** `quickshell/materials/` holds the painters, one pure-JavaScript
library shared by the shell, the Node tests and a Chromium harness:

- Gold foil on Light: four tones, three sheen bands (u = 0.42, 0.78, 1.06), glints, eased
  sheen (0.25 + 0.75 × level), burnished edge that fades out from 95 % and is gone at 100 %.
- Copper foil oxidising to verdigris on Network, four patina stages, darkest copper kept out
  of text areas; continuous across the back.
- Silver foil on the new device card.
- Level fills solid to the level mark and dithering outward past it; Legibility adds a 1 px
  line at the mark, Accessibility a solid edge.
- Bands, the Bluetooth chin bleed, tonal back interiors, rounded card and art corners (5.5 %
  of the short side), the dog-ear corner cut away with a shaded flap, rounded tip and sharp
  crease ends.
- The low-charge numeral's dithered cardstock halo (distance field, 2 px core, 22 px falloff).
- The focus light as a dithered halo (core, thick or solid by option) instead of two rectangles.

Each face is one Canvas painted into device pixels and then left as a texture; level changes
repaint at most ~30 times a second. `CardSurface`, `CardFill` and `Bands` are gone.

**Rail** (`rail/Rail.qml`). Floating vellum strip with frayed edges, fibre texture, baked
shadow and the accent wash (sage by default, gated so ink passes 4.5:1). Launcher, workspaces
as period numerals with the brass loupe, illuminated initials L C B P for the hands, clipboard,
N, removable media, tray, time over date. Hover tags in Alegreya. Hands are dealt sideways out
of the rail at the initial's height (`Geometry.dealLeft`).

**Dock** (`rail/Dock.qml`). Every window in opening order: large icon, workspace numeral
under it, none when minimised. Focus is a brass loupe on the icon. Click focuses (a minimised
window comes here first); Shift-click or middle-click queues windows that come here, in click
order, when Shift is released. Permanent or auto-hide per output (`dock.mode`, `[dock.outputs]`;
`auto` hides on outputs ≤ 800 px tall); Accessibility forces permanent. Minimise is
`illuminated minimize` (IPC `shell minimize`): the window moves to `special:minimized`.

**Space.** Rail and dock place themselves explicitly and ignore exclusive zones; two invisible
reservation surfaces hold the space (72 px each). Placement no longer depends on which
surface the compositor arranged first. Rail and dock are on the Top layer (the v6 bar was
Overlay), so fullscreen windows cover them.

**Removable media** (`services/RemovableMedia.qml`, `rail/MediaBay.qml`, `media/`). udisks2
via `udisksctl monitor` (events) and `busctl --json=short … GetManagedObjects` (snapshots), udev
for VID:PID and phones. Fourteen device kinds at life size from the output's physical size
(or a preset), unmounted faint at the screen edge, pivoting in when mounted, footprint opening
above the tray after ~300 ms. First click mounts; left-click on a mounted device opens it
(`xdg-open`) with a bounce; right-click hands it to the cards layer with its silver-foil card:
Open, Eject, Rename, Format, Properties. Format confirms by flipping (Shut Down template).
LUKS volumes arrive sealed in an envelope; the card asks for the passphrase, which reaches
`udisksctl unlock --key-file /dev/stdin` on stdin only. Scrolling over a device cycles.
`[media.overrides]` maps a VID:PID to an art kind (your e-reader).

**Reading presets.** `[reading] preset = default | legibility | accessibility` over the options
in `[reading.options]`, each `"preset"` unless set (24 at v7; five unimplemented ones and the v6
`appearance.highLegibility`/`deck.rotation` shims were removed afterwards, see CHANGELOG).
`appearance.colourVision` adds the protan/deutan muted-fill hatch and patina rim. Workspace
numerals follow `numerals`; card tilt follows `rotation` (4° / 2° / grid-aligned).

**Also.** Clipboard history sheet (cliphist; entries restored through `wl-copy`). Tray sheet
lists every item. Power Profile card (provisional: click cycles). Herbarium sheet: mode,
preset, colour vision, wash, logical pixel, dock mode, media size. `illuminated doctor` checks
the faces and the new commands. Default binds: SUPER+A deals Levels, SUPER+SHIFT+Q Power.
Blackletter defaults to Pirata One (Germania One for Legibility).

## Testing on the Inspiron, in order

1. Back up `~/.config/illuminated` and keep Waybar runnable. Install side by side as before
   (TESTING-GUIDE.md §1–3). `illuminated doctor` should list udisksctl, busctl, udevadm,
   wl-copy and the four faces.
2. `illuminated isolate chrome` (rail and dock only). Check: the rail strip, wash, fray and
   shadow; initials; the loupe over the active workspace and its slide; hover tags appear to
   the right of the rail, not clipped.
3. Workspaces: switch with your binds; the loupe follows; named workspaces show the name on hover.
4. Dock: open several windows across workspaces; order after `qs` restart is the **(3)** item
   below. Click a window on another workspace; it switches and focuses. Minimise one with
   `illuminated minimize`, then click it: it returns here. Hold Shift, click two, release.
5. If the dispatches do nothing, run `hyprctl dispatch 'hl.dsp.focus({ window = "address:0x…" })'`
   with an address from `hyprctl clients` and adjust `[dock.dispatch]` (the move template is the
   least certain: see below).
6. Full shell: `illuminated start`. Click L, C, B, P: hands slide in from the left beside the
   rail. Drag Light: the gold sheen strengthens with level, the burnish disappears at 100 %.
   Connect/disconnect Wi-Fi for the copper stages.
7. Removable media: insert a USB stick. It appears faint at the edge above the tray. Click:
   it mounts and pivots in, rail items above move up. Click again: file manager opens.
   Right-click: card. Try Properties, Rename, Eject (it slides out). Pull one out without
   ejecting: a warning tag. Insert a LUKS stick: sealed; unlock from the card.
8. Herbarium sheet: switch presets and colour vision; check the rail numerals (capital under
   Legibility, Arabic under Accessibility), dock forced permanent under Accessibility.
9. Measure: `smem`/PSS of the quickshell process before and after opening each hand, and idle
   CPU with the rail up (DESIGN.md §10). Report the first paint time of a hand.

  busctl/udev/df parsers, dock window model and dispatch strings, QML structure (delimiters,

- `python3 -m unittest`: config, installer, wrapper, greeter.
- `tests/visual/render.cjs`: every painter rendered in Chromium through a stand-in that
  exposes only QML's Context2D API (it throws on anything QML lacks); contact sheets in
  `artifacts/v7/`.
- The runtime suites (`dev/test-all.sh`) were updated for the rail (namespace
  `illuminated-rail`, exclusive zone 72, rail-hand deployment) but **not run**.

Needs verification on hardware (3):

- QML load: no qmllint or Quickshell here. Expect first-run fixes of the kind v4/v5 needed.
- Canvas cost under QML's V4 engine: a card face is 2–18 ms in Chromium; expect several times
  that in V4. First deal of a hand may hitch; device bakes are similar.
- Canvas at fractional scale: the Canvas is sized in device pixels and scaled down; Qt's own
  Canvas DPR handling may resample `putImageData`. Check grain crispness at 1.25×.
- Hyprland Lua dispatch: `hl.dsp.focus({ window = "address:…" })` is confirmed from community
  configs; the `window =` and `follow = false` fields of `hl.dsp.window.move` are not. Check
  `/usr/share/hypr/stubs/` and adjust `[dock.dispatch]`.
- Dock order after a shell restart (Hyprland's toplevel list order is assumed to be opening order).
- busctl's JSON shape for `a{oa{sa{sv}}}` (the parser accepts wrapped and plain values).
- Physical size: `ShellScreen.physicalPixelDensity` (px/mm) / devicePixelRatio as life size.
- Phones: `mtp://<ID_SERIAL>/` and `afc://<serial>/` URIs depend on gvfs/kio.
- PopupWindow tags over a layer surface (positioning, focus).

## Runtime report (2026-11-02) and fixes

First real run: Quickshell 0.3.2, Qt 6.12.0, headless Sway, software rendering (report in
the test bundle; Hyprland could not start headless there).

Fixed in this build:
- **ClipboardPage stopped CardsLayer loading** (`contentItem.horizontalAlignment` on a Button).
  ActionButton now exposes `horizontalAlignment`. Fix from the tester's patch.
- **Three-argument `putImageData` draws nothing on Qt 6.12's Canvas**, so every painted
  material (rail, initials, cards, loupe, halo, devices) was missing. All sites now pass the
  dirty rectangle; the Chromium stand-in rejects the three-argument form and a static test
  checks every call. Fix from the tester's patch.
- **Wallpaper left the bottom of the screen bare** (a translated PreserveAspectCrop). The image
  is now sized to cover the output and offset by `focusY`.
- The hover-tag id shadowed the `tag` property it was bound to (qmllint `id-shadows-member`);
  renamed. `anchor.updateAnchor()` is called only where it exists.
- The developer image installs udisks2, wl-clipboard, cliphist and xdg-utils; `test-all.sh`
  says its lint step is non-strict.

With the patch, artifacts, smoke, audio, reload, greeter, single-config and session-UI pass.

Still open:
- **Foundation suite:** the launcher reopen / launch-marker steps fail intermittently. Not
  diagnosed. Hypothesis (3): first paints of the rail, vellum and wallpaper are synchronous
  JavaScript on the GUI thread, so under software rendering the launcher's close animation and
  surface teardown finish later than the test's 0.65 s wait. To bisect: rerun with
  `features.wallpaper = false`, then `features.rail = false`; time the first `onPainting` of
  `Vellum`. If confirmed, it is a real cost on the Inspiron too and the first paints should be
  deferred until after the first frame.
- Fonts are not in the developer image (Pirata One missing in logs).
- Live Hyprland: dispatch, reservations, fullscreen, real removable media, fractional scale,
  performance.

## Second pass (2026-11-02): rail objects, sheets, prompts, slips, launcher, lock

Still **not loaded under Quickshell here**. Static checks, Node and Python tests and the
Chromium renders (`artifacts/v7/objects-*.png`) pass; everything else below is (3) until you
run it.

**Rail objects** (`materials/RailObjects.js`, `rail/RailObject.qml`, `rail/Correspondence.qml`).
The illuminated initials are replaced by life-size objects, top to bottom: the printed tray
mark, the nib (launcher, top-down: steel with a gold centre band and tip), workspaces, vial
(Levels: iron-gall ink line = volume, empty when muted), antenna end (Connections: one wave per
signal stage, first dashed while connecting), converter (Battery: lying at 52° so the whole
reservoir shows at life size; iron-gall ink = charge; canary bands while charging, a canary
collar on the nozzle when held),
matchbox (Power), then removable media and the clock. At the foot, a sheet's folded corner
comes in from the screen's bottom-left corner with a paperclip: clicking on or near the clip
deals clipboard history, anywhere else on the paper deals notifications; unread notifications
show as handwriting. A hand group you define yourself keeps an illuminated initial. The
converter's slot is the tallest on the rail (~114 px on the Inspiron).

**Sheets unfurl flat** (`artifacts/VellumSheet.qml`). The vellum is painted once at full size
and revealed by a straight edge moving away from the rail (from the left beside the rail,
from the top otherwise). No roll edge. The vellum is painted on first open, not at startup.
Buttons, labels, fields and sliders (`components/`) are printed on the vellum: Alegreya on a
hairline rule, the focus halo for keyboard focus. Every sheet (mixer, network, Bluetooth,
media, history, tray, clipboard, settings) uses them.

**Prompt sheet** (`prompts/`). One centred, solid-edged sheet over a light scrim, keyboard
held exclusively, Escape cancels. Used by:
- the **polkit agent** (`polkit.qml`, `features.polkit`, off by default). Only one agent can
  register per session: stop your current agent before turning it on. `illuminated status`
  reports `polkitRegistered`. To go back, turn it off and start the old agent again; nothing
  is removed.
- the **Wi-Fi passphrase** (network sheet → *Connect with passphrase…*). The field is cleared
  as soon as the answer is sent; NetworkManager decides whether to store it.

**Notification slips** (`notifications.qml`). Cardstock slips slide out from behind the
rail's edge near its foot, newest lowest, stacking upward (from the right edge when the rail
is off). App name in the card face; summary, body and action buttons in Alegreya. Urgency is
an edge band plus the word *Urgent*; critical slips stay until dismissed.

**Launcher spread** (`tools/Launcher.qml`, `Geometry.spread`). One vellum spread centred in
the space right of the rail and below the dock, unfurling flat from its left edge (no
centring phase). Left page: blackletter *Search* field and six pin plates (3 × 2,
placeholder art: the icon over a pale accent ground) from `launcher.pins`, Alt+1…6. Right
page: all applications, names in the card face, the selection under the focus halo; the
description shows on the selected row. Default width 820.

**Lock screen** (`lock.qml`, `features.lock`, opt-in). `WlSessionLock` + `PamContext`
(`lock.pam`, default `login`). Rules:
1. Only `PamResult.Success` unlocks. Failed, Error, MaxTries and PAM errors keep it locked and
   start a new conversation after a growing delay.
2. The only other unlock is `abandon`, which drops a lock the compositor has **not** yet
   confirmed (`secure` false), so the fallback can take over.
3. If Quickshell dies while locked, the compositor keeps the screen covered.
The loader is latched: a config reload never unloads the lock.

To use it: `features.lock = true` and `commands.lock = ["illuminated", "lock"]` (apply warns
until you do). `illuminated lock` asks the shell to lock and returns once `secure`; if the
shell is not running, the feature is off, or confirmation takes longer than `lock.timeout`
(3000 ms), it runs `lock.fallback` (`swaylock --daemonize`). Sleep therefore still never
suspends an unlocked session. The fallback must return once locked (hyprlock is refused).

**Recovering from a crashed lock** (3): Hyprland shows its "lock screen app died" screen. From
a TTY (Ctrl+Alt+F3), `hyprctl --instance 0 dispatch exec swaylock` starts a new locker if the
compositor allows session-lock restore (`misc.allow_session_lock_restore`; check its Lua name
in `/usr/share/hypr/stubs/`). Set that before relying on the shell lock.

## Skins (2026-11-02)

Everything visual on the rail and the dock, plus removable media, card art, launcher pins and
the material textures, now comes from a skin: `skin.json` plus images and sprite sheets.
The format is in [SKINS.md](SKINS.md); it replaces the earlier per-object art folders.

**What the shell does now.** It no longer draws objects: it loads, composites, dithers and
shadows images. The old drawings live in `scripts/skin/painters/` and only generate the
default skin (`node scripts/skin/build-materials.cjs`, then `node scripts/skin/build-default.cjs`).

**Buttons.** Every element defines:
- its looks (images, sheet cells, frames chosen by shell values, iron-gall ink through a mask,
  markers);
- regions (sub-buttons);
- active areas (alpha, rect, circle, polygon, mask image, with padding for thin shapes);
- frame and transform animations on hover, press, release, click, load and value changes;
- triggers (click, right, middle, double, scroll up/down).

**Actions.** Triggers name actions from one registry (`skin/Actions.qml`). `command:<name>` runs
an argv defined only in config.toml `[commands.custom]`; a skin never supplies one.

**Layout and additions.**
- The rail's order (elements, workspaces, hands without elements, media, clock, a foot
  element at the screen corner) comes from the skin.
- So do the dock's elements before and after the windows.
- Skins can add cards (`skin:<id>`) and hands; config groups may list skin cards too.

**Materials.** The vellum texture, fray threshold, foil sheen profile and glints, patina noise
and tone ramps are images (`materials/`), decoded once into fields that the painters sample.

**Example.** `quickshell/skins/example/` is a worked example. It puts the tray on the paper,
adds a wax seal that deals a new hand with a new card, a dock button, and hover animations.

**New files and what replaced them.**
- New: `quickshell/skin/` (`Skin.js` the pure format: merge, images, conditions, looks,
  animation timing, hits, actions, validation; `Compose.js` look → pixels; `SkinStore`,
  `ShellState`, `Actions`, `Registry.js`, `SkinElement`, `SkinCard`) and
  `materials/MaterialFields.qml`.
- Removed: `RailObject.qml`, `Correspondence.qml`, `RailObjects.js`, `ArtSpec.qml`,
  `services/ArtLibrary.qml`, `art-templates/`, `docs/ART.md`.

**Verified here.**
- Node tests for the format, compose and validity of the default skin (every image present,
  every action known).
- The Chromium harness renders the rail through the same compose path as the shell
  (`artifacts/v7/skin-*.png`, including the example), hit-tests the foot (clip, tray, sheet,
  outside), and renders cards, chrome and devices on the material images.

**Needs a real run (3).** See SKINS.md, *Verify on hardware*. In particular:
- 9-argument `drawImage` for sheet cells;
- `?v=` on `file://` URLs;
- the one-time material decode on the rail's canvas;
- `Loader`-switched skin cards in the deck.

   InitialMark; `Materials.js` is a re-export facade for tests and tools only. Shell files import
   the specific module.
3. **Procedural leftovers — mostly done.** Initial ground, loupes (with glass masks) and the
   selection ring are skin images. Left: the focus halo and the card band pattern.
4. **Opening with a screen y — done.** Deals, history, clipboard and sheets go through
   `Actions.run`.
5. **Rail initials — partly.** The ground is a skin image; the letter stays live text. Hands that
   want full art use an element.
6. **Card faces — declined.** ServiceCard faces are level-driven material logic plus service
   wiring (PipeWire, UPower, NM…). As skin cards they would need the material model inside the
   skin format. Their art is already skin `cardArt`; new cards use `skin:<id>`.
7. **Two skin validators — done.** `scripts/skin/check.cjs` runs `Skin.check` on the merged skin;
   `illuminated doctor` calls it when node is installed and keeps its file/command checks.
8. **Painters in two places — done.** `DeviceGeometry.js` holds dimensions and placement; shell
   `DeviceArt.js` is images and bake; the generator painter takes its dimensions from it.
9. **Skin-card loading — done.** `artifacts/CardKinds.js` `family(kind)` picks the component in
   CardsLayer.
