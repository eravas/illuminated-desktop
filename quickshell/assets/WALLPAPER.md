# wallpaper.jpg

Claude Monet, *Water Lily Pond* (1900), oil on canvas. Public domain (Monet died 1926).
Source: Art Institute of Chicago, open access (CC0). 3000 × 2668 px as downloaded;
converted to JPEG (quality 92).

Shown by `wallpaper.qml` on the background layer of every output, cropped to fill
(`wallpaper.focusY` sets the vertical crop; the bridge sits about 40 % down). Set
`[wallpaper] path` to use another image, or `features.wallpaper = false` to keep your own
wallpaper tool.
